## Decision

Joey, 2026-10-10, on what "put skin in the game" meant: "paid tools and data not real betting money I wont do that
until the model is guaranteed proven."

1. Paid tools and data are in scope when they would measurably help prove or improve a strategy. The assistant
   recommends them with cost and expected benefit; the purchase is Joey's.
2. The trader stays on fake money. No code that places real orders, opens an account or handles exchange credentials
   (the rule already in `CLAUDE.md`), until a strategy has passed its pre-registered forward test
   (`docs/EXPERIMENTS.md`) and Joey then asks for it explicitly.
3. "Proven" means passing that test: strong evidence, never a guarantee. A real-money proposal will show the arithmetic
   first: capital, measured edge after fees, and how much the markets can absorb.

## Why

- The research (`docs/research/15-who-makes-the-money.md`) found eight-figure results came from tens of millions at
  risk with private information or industrial speed, and that players just as large lost. The one edge measured here
  (mention markets, +15–18% after fees on 7,929 past markets) is real on history but unproven forward and limited by
  thin markets.
- Paper trading finds and proves edges at no cost; real money adds risk before there is evidence.

## Alternatives rejected

- **Real money now, small stakes:** risk with no proven edge, and real fills would complicate the forward test.
- **No spending at all:** some data (for example sharper sportsbook lines) can only be tested with a paid plan.

## Risk

A real edge could fade or be arbitraged away while it is proven on paper. Accepted: proof first.

## Reversibility

Joey's call at any time; the code rule in `CLAUDE.md` changes only on his explicit request.
